# Season Rules

Permanent Rugby365 season standards. See also the [Rule Book](./rule-book.md).

## Club competitions

Cross-year seasons (`2025–26`). Window ≈ August–July. Examples: Premiership, URC, Top 14, Champions Cup.

## International competitions

Calendar-year seasons (`2026`). Examples: Six Nations, Rugby Championship, July Internationals, Autumn Nations Series.

## Tournament competitions

Tournament year (`2027`). Examples: Rugby World Cup, British & Irish Lions, U20 World Cup.

## Resolve inputs

Competition · Season type · Start/end · Provider mapping · Fixture date.

Never calendar year alone. Unmapped → `SEASON_UNMAPPED` → review.
